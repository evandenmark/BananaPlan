# NNNN. Short imperative title

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [NNNN](NNNN-....md)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Affects:** the files, tables, or subsystems this constrains

## Context

What situation forced a choice? Include the constraint that made it non-obvious —
a limit of the platform, a property of the domain, an incident. A reader who
knows nothing about the day this was written should understand the pressure.

## Decision

What was decided, stated as a rule someone can follow or violate. Present tense.

## Consequences

What this makes easy, and what it makes hard or impossible. Name the cost
honestly — the cost is the reason the next person needs this document.

## Alternatives considered

- **The alternative** — why it lost. One line each is fine.

If this section is empty, reconsider whether there was a decision here at all.
